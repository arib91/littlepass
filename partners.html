How it works

1Add your classes

Ages, days and times, how many spots you want to offer, and your price per child.

2Families book

They find you by their child's age, neighborhood and day, book with credits, and get a reminder the evening before.

3You get paid monthly

Your full price for every completed booking, at the end of each month.

What you get

💵 100% of your price

You set what you receive per child. LittlePass keeps the difference between what families pay and your price, never a cut of it.

🎛️ You control your spots

Offer only the seats you'd otherwise leave empty. Change spots and times any time.

✅ Paid for no-shows

Families can cancel free up to 24 hours before. After that the booking stands, and you're paid.

🗂️ Less admin

Bookings, reminders, waitlists, refunds and cancellations are handled in the app. You get an email for each booking.

📝 Your waiver, signed first

Families read and sign your waiver in the app before they can book, and you see who signed.

⭐ Your own studio page

Photos, class details and reviews from families who actually attended. Your phone and arrival notes go only to booked families.

What we ask of studios

  • Sign the Studio Partner Agreement
  • Carry general liability insurance and share proof
  • Send a W-9 so we can pay you
  • Keep class details and spots up to date, and honor every booking

Questions studios ask

Will this undercut my own memberships?

Offer drop-in or trial spots only. Families often try a class through LittlePass and then sign up with you directly.

Can I keep my booking software?

Yes. Hold back the spots you give LittlePass; your dashboard shows who's coming.

What does it cost me?

Nothing. No sign-up fee, no monthly fee, no commission on your price.

How do payouts work?

Once a month, for every completed booking. We're moving to automatic payouts straight to your bank account.

Can I stop any time?

Yes. You'd just honor the bookings already made.
